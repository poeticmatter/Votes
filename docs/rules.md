# Votes: how to play

*The rules as the game plays them now, in plain language. They start from the designer's [Draft 5.13](rules-draft-5.13.md), with players' own tiles, Factories that draw cubes from the bag, people moving only when players pull them, and two printed Factories. This file also covers the calls the game makes where the rules leave a question open.*

Votes is a game for 3 to 5 players. You build up five small towns until they grow into each other. Every time two towns join, the new city holds an election, and you score for the seats your people hold. Most points at the end wins.

## What's on the table

- **A map of hexes.** Some are mountains, and nothing can go on a mountain. Five towns are marked on it, each with a **starting Residence**. Two of them, E and B, also have a **Factory** printed beside them. The printed tiles belong to nobody.
- **Each player's own tiles:** 15 **Residences**, 5 **Factories** and 1 **School**. A tile you place stays yours.
- **People:** cubes in each player's colour, plus grey cubes. Grey people live on the board like anyone else, but they never vote.
- **A bag** that cubes are drawn from.

## Setup

1. Take 2 cubes of each player's colour and add grey cubes until there are 10. Mix them and put 2 at random on each starting Residence.
2. Do the same again for the printed Factories: 2 of each player's colour, grey up to 10. Take 1 grey out for each printed Factory and put it on the right of that town's starting Residence, so E's and B's start with 3 people. Mix the rest and put 4 at random on each Factory.
   - With 5 players there's no grey among the Factories' cubes: the two towns' greys come from the supply, and each Factory gets 5.
3. Put 4 cubes of each player's colour in the bag and add grey cubes until there are 20.
4. Each player takes their tiles. A first player is picked at random. Play goes around the table from there.

So with 3 or 4 players, 40 cubes start the game: 12 in the starting Residences, 8 on the printed Factories and 20 in the bag.

## Words you'll need

- A **city** is a group of tiles that touch each other. At the start, each town is a city of its own (with its printed Factory, if it has one).
- Residences have no limit; people keep piling in.
- People in a Residence sit **in a row**. Newcomers join on the right. When someone leaves, it's the leftmost person of their colour.
- Cubes on a **Factory** aren't people yet. They wait there until a Residence pulls them in.
- **Nobody moves by themselves.** People only move when a player pulls them, or arrive from the bag after a merger.

## Your turn

1. **Place a tile** from your own supply on an empty hex that touches any tile. If none of your tiles fits anywhere, you pass this step.
2. **The tile does its work** (see The tiles).
3. **Merger.** If the tile touched two or more cities, they are now one city. It holds an election (see below).
4. **Pull people**, as many as you like, one at a time (see Pulling). Then end your turn.

You make every choice on your turn, whoever the cubes belong to.

## The tiles

### Residence
A seat. Only you can pull people into your own Residences. Anyone can pull into a starting Residence.

### Factory
When you place it, it **draws 5 cubes from the bag** onto itself, or as many as the bag holds. Every seat in its city scores 1 more point at elections.

### School
Residences in its city may pull people from **other cities** as well as their own. You can't place a School touching a city that already has one, so it can't join one either; two Schools can only share a city after a merger.

## Pulling

After placing, you may pull people one at a time into your own Residences and the starting ones, for as long as you like:

- **From a Factory**, while the Residence holds **fewer than 3** people. The Factory's owner adds **1 of their cubes to the bag**. For a printed Factory, which nobody owns, a **grey** cube goes in instead.
- **From another Residence** that holds **at least 2 more** people than the one pulling.

Both come from the Residence's own city. A Residence in a city with a **School** may also pull from any other city, by the same rules. Each person who comes from another city has the **School's owner add 1 of their cubes to the bag**. If the city's Schools belong to different players, the player pulling chooses which of them adds the cube.

## Merging and elections

When a tile you place touches two or more cities, they become one city at once. Then the merged city, and only that city, holds an **election**:

- **Every Residence is a seat**, whoever owns it. The player colour with the most people there wins it. If colours tie, the seat goes to the tied colour furthest left in the row. Grey people never vote. A seat with nobody voting (empty, or all grey) belongs to nobody.
- **Every seat scores its holder 1 point for each Factory in the city**, printed Factories included.

After the vote, **each Residence in the merged city draws 1 person from the bag**, in order across the map, until the bag runs out. Only then does **each player add 1 cube of their colour to the bag for every seat they won**, so none of those cubes can arrive in the same turn. Then you pull as usual. After the last merger the game simply ends.

## End of the game

The game ends after the election that leaves **one city**, or when everyone has passed in a row because none of their tiles fit. Most points wins. If points tie, the player with the most people on the board wins, and anyone still tied shares the win.

## Playtest dials

When you create a game you can change the numbers in the lobby's "Playtest dials": how many tiles of each kind each player has, how many cubes of each player's colour start in the bag, and how many cubes the bag starts with in all (grey makes up the rest).
