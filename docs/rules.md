# Votes: how to play

*The rules as the game plays them now, in plain language. The designer's draft is [rules-draft-5.13.md](rules-draft-5.13.md); this file also covers the calls the game makes where the draft leaves a question open.*

Votes is a game for 3 to 5 players. You build up five small towns until they grow into each other. Every time two towns join, the new city holds an election, and you score for the seats your people hold. Most points at the end wins.

## What's on the table

- **A map of hexes.** Some are mountains, and nothing can go on a mountain. Five towns are marked on it, each with a **starting Residence**. One town also has a **Factory** printed beside its Residence.
- **Tiles** in a shared supply: 15 **Residences**, 10 **Factories** and 5 **Schools**.
- **People:** cubes in each player's colour, plus 10 grey cubes. Grey people live on the board like anyone else, but they never vote.
- **A bag** that people are drawn from.

## Setup

1. Take 2 people of each player's colour and add grey people until there are 10. Mix them and put 2 at random on each starting Residence.
2. Put the grey people that are left in the bag, along with 2 more people of each player's colour.
3. Each player takes 2 Residence tiles from the shared supply into their own supply.
4. A first player is picked at random. Play goes around the table from there.

## Words you'll need

- A **city** is a group of tiles that touch each other. At the start, each town is a city of its own.
- A Residence with **4 or more people** is **overcrowded**. Residences have no limit; people keep piling in.
- People in a Residence sit **in a row**. Newcomers join on the right. When someone leaves, it's the leftmost person of their colour.

## Your turn

Do **one** of these:

1. **Take a tile:** take a Factory or a School from the shared supply into your own. You can't take Residences. You only get those at setup and by placing Factories.
2. **Place a tile** from your own supply on an empty hex that touches any tile.
   - Residences and Factories go down **face down**. A face-down tile is a promise (see below).
   - Schools go down **face up**. You can't place a School touching a city that already has one.
3. **Flip a tile:** turn any face-down tile face up, whoever placed it.

If you can't do any of these, you pass.

## The tiles

### Residence
- **Placed face down:** for each overcrowded Residence in its city, add 1 of your people to the bag. A face-down Residence holds nobody and isn't a seat yet.
- **Flipped:** it becomes a seat, and people move in (see Moving).

### Factory
- **Placed face down:** take 2 Residence tiles from the shared supply into yours, or as many as are left.
- **Flipped:** draw 1 person from the bag **for every city** on the board. Each goes to the least crowded Residence in that city. If Residences tie, you choose. Every person who arrives adds 1 of your people to the bag. If the bag runs out, nobody else comes.

### School
- **Placed:** its city now pulls people in from cities without a School (see Moving).
- **Flipped into a University:** only possible in a city that has two Schools, which only happens when two School cities merge. A University city also pulls from cities that have a School but no University.

## Moving

People move after a **flip** or after **placing a School**, and at no other time. Moves happen one person at a time, until nobody can move:

1. **Levelling first:** a person moves to another Residence in the same city that has at least 2 fewer people.
2. **Then School pulls:** a city with a School pulls people from Residences in cities without one, into a Residence with fewer people (1 fewer is enough). A University city also pulls from School cities.

The most crowded Residence always gives first, to the least crowded one. If there's still a choice, or if several colours could move, you choose. When only one move is possible it happens by itself. Moving never adds anyone to the bag.

## Merging and elections

When a tile you place touches two or more cities, they become one city at once. Face-down tiles count too. Any moving that turn happens in the merged city. Then the merged city, and only that city, holds an **election**:

- **Every face-up Residence is a seat.** The player colour with the most people there wins it. If colours tie, the seat goes to the tied colour furthest left in the row. Grey people never vote. A seat with nobody voting (empty, or all grey) belongs to nobody.
- **Every seat scores its holder 1 point for each face-up Factory in the city.** Face-down Factories count for nothing.

## End of the game

The game ends after the election that leaves **one city**, or when everyone has passed in a row because nobody can do anything. Most points wins. If points tie, the player with the most people on the board wins, and anyone still tied shares the win.

## Rule set

When you create a game, the lobby's "Rule set" picks which rules it plays. **Draft 5.13** is everything above. **Round 3 playtest** tries proposed rules instead, and changes the game like this:

- **Your own tiles.** There is no shared supply. Everyone starts with 8 Residences, 2 Factories, 1 School and 1 Town Hall of their own.
- **A turn is placing one tile, face up.** Nobody takes or flips tiles, so there are no face-down tiles, promises or Universities. A placed tile does at once what flipping it would have done.
- **Placing adds your people to the bag:** 1 for a Residence, 2 for a Factory, none for a School or Town Hall. People arriving add nobody.
- **A Factory brings 2 people from the bag for its own city**, each into the least crowded Residence there. You choose between Residences that tie.
- **A Town Hall's city holds an election** when it's placed, even if it joins no cities.
- **Catch-up people.** Players after the first start with extra people of their colour in the bag: 1 for the second player and 2 for each player after.

## Playtest dials

When you create a game you can change the numbers in the lobby's "Playtest dials": the people of each colour in the bag at the start, the number of grey people, and, with Draft 5.13, how many tiles of each kind are in the supply.
