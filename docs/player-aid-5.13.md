# Votes — Player aid (Draft 5.13)

*A reminder, not the rules: `docs/rules-draft-5.13.md` is the source of truth. This stands in for the asset generator's printed player aid (still 5.11) until 5.13 is signed off.*

## Your turn: one action
| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take** | A Factory or School from the shared supply into your supply. |
| **Place** | A tile from your supply on an empty open hex touching a tile. Residences and Factories go **face down**; Schools face up. |
| **Flip** | Any face-down tile, anyone's; or a School into a University in a city with two Schools. |

Then: **movement** after a flip or a School placement, and a **merger** with an election if your placement joined cities.

## Tiles
| Tile | Placed | Flipped |
|---|---|---|
| 🏠 Residence | Face down. +1 of your cubes in the bag per overcrowded Residence (4+) in its city. Not a seat. | A seat. People level into it. |
| 🏭 Factory | Face down. Take 2 Residence tiles into your supply. | 1 person from the bag per city, into its least crowded Residence. +1 cube per arrival. Scores. |
| 🏫 School | Face up. Its city pulls from cities with no School. One per city. | University (city with two Schools). Also pulls from School cities without one. |

## Movement (after a flip or a School placement)
1. **Levelling first:** to another Residence in the same city with **2+ fewer** people.
2. **Then School pulls:** from a city that ranks lower (no School < School < University) into a Residence with **fewer** people.
3. Most crowded source first, into the least crowded destination. Ties and which cube: you choose.
4. Until nobody can move. Moving adds no cubes.

## Elections (the newly merged city)
- Each **face-up** Residence is a seat. Most player cubes wins; greys never vote; ties go to the leftmost player colour.
- Each seat scores its holder **1 point per face-up Factory** in the city.

## Game end
After the 4th election, or once a full round passes with nobody able to act. Most points wins; tiebreak, most cubes on the board.
